Your online presentation with Hype Vision

A short, practical session: we show you live what AI can detect with the cameras you already have, and you decide whether it is worth a closer look.

What we'll cover

  1. Your site — a few questions about your plant, your current cameras and where safety or production visibility is hardest today.
  2. Live demo — the platform on real footage: alerts, the live plant map, station and line reports, and asking the assistant questions in plain language.
  3. Fit for your cameras — which detections are realistic on your setup, and which are not.
  4. Next step — if it makes sense, how a small pilot on a handful of cameras would work. No commitment.

What it can do on existing CCTV

SafetyPPE, restricted and hazardous zones, forklift–pedestrian proximity, falls, smoke and fire.
ProductivityLine stoppages, idle and cycle time per station, OEE availability, dock times.
QualitySurface defects, missing parts, labels and fill levels, counting on the line.

Helpful to have ready (optional)

  • Roughly how many IP cameras you have and, if known, their brand (any RTSP/ONVIF camera works).
  • One or two situations you would most like to catch or measure.
  • A colleague from HSE, production or IT, if they would like to join.

If you'd like, you can share a short sample clip from one of your cameras after the call, and we'll tell you which detections would work on it.

Privacy by design

No facial recognition — we analyse events, not people. With an on-premise deployment, video never leaves your building, and reports are per station rather than per person.
